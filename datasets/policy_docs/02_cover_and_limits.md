# Cover and annual limits

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.
> All amounts in EUR.

Essential Plus pays up to a set amount for each visit or item, and up to a set
number of visits or items in each policy year.

| Treatment | Most we pay per visit or item | Visits or items per policy year | What you also need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GP visit | 60 | 8 visits | Nothing extra |
| Consultant visit | 120 | 4 visits | A referral from your GP |
| Physiotherapy | 50 per session | 10 sessions | A referral from a GP or consultant |
| Prescription | 40 per item | 12 items | Nothing extra |
| Day case procedure | 1,500 per procedure | 2 procedures | A pre-authorisation number |
| Inpatient stay | Assessed individually | Assessed individually | A pre-authorisation number |

**At the limit.** A claim for exactly the limit, for example 60 for a GP visit,
is within the limit.

**Reaching your annual limit.** Once you have used all the visits or items for
a treatment type in a policy year, further claims of that type in the same
policy year are not paid. For example, after 8 paid GP visits, a ninth GP visit
in the same policy year is not covered.

**Above the limit.** If a receipt is for more than the limit, the claim is
reviewed by a claims assessor. Any claim for more than 1,000 is also reviewed
by an assessor.
