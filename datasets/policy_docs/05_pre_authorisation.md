# Pre-authorisation

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.
> All amounts in EUR.

Some treatment must be approved by Harbourlight Health before it takes place.
When it is approved, you receive a **pre-authorisation number**. Include this
number when you claim.

**Treatment that needs pre-authorisation:**

| Treatment | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Day case procedure (treatment in hospital without an overnight stay) | 1,500 per procedure, 2 procedures per policy year |
| Inpatient stay (one or more nights in hospital) | Assessed individually |

**If the number is missing.** A day case or inpatient claim without a
pre-authorisation number is reviewed by a claims assessor and is not paid
automatically.

**Inpatient stays are always reviewed.** Every inpatient claim is reviewed by
a claims assessor, even when it has a pre-authorisation number.

**Other treatment.** GP visits, consultant visits, physiotherapy and
prescriptions do not need pre-authorisation, but consultant visits and
physiotherapy need a referral. See "Cover and annual limits".
