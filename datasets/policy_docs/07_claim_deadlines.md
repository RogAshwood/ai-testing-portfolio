# Claim deadlines

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.

You must submit a claim **within 12 months of the treatment date**.

**The last day to claim** is the same calendar date one year after the
treatment. For example, for treatment on 10 March 2027, a claim submitted on
10 March 2028 is on time. A claim submitted on 11 March 2028 is late.

**Treatment on 29 February.** The deadline is 28 February of the following
year. For example, for treatment on 29 February 2028, the last day to claim is
28 February 2029.

**Late claims** are normally declined. You can appeal a decline; see "Appeals
and complaints".

**Tip.** Claim as soon as you have your receipt, so that a missing document
can be sorted out well before the deadline.
