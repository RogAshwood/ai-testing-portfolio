# How to make a claim

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.

**What to send with every claim:**

1. An **itemised receipt** from the provider, showing the treatment, the date
   and the amount. Every claim needs one; a payment card slip or bank
   statement is not an itemised receipt.
2. A **referral**, if the treatment needs one:
   - consultant visits need a referral from your GP;
   - physiotherapy needs a referral from a GP or consultant.
3. A **pre-authorisation number**, for a day case procedure or an inpatient
   stay.

**Claims without a receipt.** If the itemised receipt is missing, the claim is
not paid automatically. A claims assessor reviews it and may contact you. Ask
your provider for a duplicate if you have lost the original.

**Who can claim.** The policyholder or a dependant named on the policy.

**When to claim.** Within 12 months of the treatment date. See "Claim
deadlines".

**After you claim.** See "What happens after you submit a claim".
