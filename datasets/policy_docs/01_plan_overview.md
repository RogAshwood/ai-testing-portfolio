# Your Essential Plus plan: overview

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.
> Not based on any real insurer or policy. All amounts in EUR.

Essential Plus is a health insurance plan from Harbourlight Health. It helps pay
for everyday and hospital treatment received in Ireland.

**Who is covered.** The policyholder and any dependants named on the policy.
Each covered person can make claims in their own name.

**Your policy year.** Limits such as "8 GP visits" apply per policy year. The
policy year starts on the date your cover began and runs for 12 months.

**What the plan pays for.** GP visits, consultant visits, physiotherapy,
prescriptions, day case procedures and inpatient stays, up to the limits set
out in "Cover and annual limits".

**What the plan does not pay for.** Some treatments are never covered. See
"What is not covered".

**When cover starts.** Most treatment has a waiting period after you join. See
"Waiting periods".

**Making a claim.** See "How to make a claim" and "Claim deadlines".
