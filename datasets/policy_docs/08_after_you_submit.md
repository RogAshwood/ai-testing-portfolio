# What happens after you submit a claim

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.
> All amounts in EUR.

1. **Checks.** We confirm your policy was active on the treatment date and
   that the person treated is covered.
2. **Automated triage.** An automated system reads the claim and recommends
   whether it can be paid straight away, needs review by a claims assessor, or
   is likely to be declined. The system only makes a recommendation.
3. **Assessor review.** A claims assessor reviews every claim that is not
   straightforward, including claims above a limit, claims for more than
   1,000, inpatient claims, claims with missing documents and claims
   recommended for decline. The assessor makes the decision and may approve
   part of a claim.
4. **Payment and notification.** We tell you the outcome and pay any approved
   amount.

Straightforward claims recommended for payment are checked by an assessor on a
sample basis.

**No outcome is guaranteed before a claim is assessed.** Staff and online help
cannot confirm in advance that a claim will be paid. The decision is made by a
claims assessor once the claim and documents have been reviewed.

If a claim is declined, you can appeal. See "Appeals and complaints".
