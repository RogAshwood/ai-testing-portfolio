# Appeals and complaints

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.

**Appealing a decline.** If a claim is declined, you can appeal. A senior
claims assessor who was not involved in the original decision reviews the
appeal.

**How to appeal.** Reply to the decision letter or use the member portal, and
say why you think the decision is wrong. Include anything that was missing
from the original claim, for example a referral, a pre-authorisation number,
or a provider's description showing an injury was accidental.

**Complaints.** If you are unhappy with our service, rather than a claim
decision, you can make a complaint through the member portal or in writing.
