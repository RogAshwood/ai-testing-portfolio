Use the member portal to submit claims, upload receipts and referrals, track a
claim, appeal a decision or make a complaint.

**The online help assistant** answers general questions about your plan from
these member documents. It cannot see your claims or your personal details,
cannot give medical advice, and cannot confirm whether a claim will be paid.

**Your data.** We use the information on a claim only to assess and pay it,
and to meet our legal obligations. Please do not type medical details into the
online help assistant.
