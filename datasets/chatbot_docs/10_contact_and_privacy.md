**Member portal:** members.harbourlight.example
**Post:** Harbourlight Health Member Services (placeholder address)

Use the member portal to submit claims, upload receipts and referrals, track a
claim, appeal a decision or make a complaint.

**The online help assistant** answers general questions about your plan from
these member documents. When you are signed in to the member portal, it can
also look up the status of your own claims. It cannot see anyone else's
claims, cannot change, approve or resubmit a claim, cannot give medical
advice, and cannot confirm whether a claim will be paid. To send documents or
appeal a decision, use the member portal.

**Your data.** We use the information on a claim only to assess and pay it,
and to meet our legal obligations. Please do not type medical details into the
online help assistant.
