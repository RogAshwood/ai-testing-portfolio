# Waiting periods

> Fictional insurer and plan, written as test data for an AI evaluation project.
> M5 version: replaces datasets/policy_docs/04_waiting_periods.md for the
> M5 chatbot only. The Day 3 chatbot still uses the original. Only the
> paragraph on how days are counted is added.

When you first join, there is a waiting period of **182 days** from the date
your cover began. Treatment in the first 182 days is not covered. Treatment on
day 182 or later is covered, subject to the usual limits.

**How the days are counted.** The date your cover began is day 0, so the
waiting period is days 0 to 181 and cover starts on day 182, which is 182 days
after your cover began. For example, if your cover began on 1 March 2026,
treatment on 29 August 2026 (day 181) is not covered and treatment on
30 August 2026 (day 182) is covered.

**Two exceptions apply from day one:**

1. **GP visits** are covered from the first day of your policy.
2. **Treatment for an accidental injury** is covered from the first day, as
   long as the receipt or description from your doctor, physiotherapist or
   hospital shows the injury was accidental, for example "X-ray following
   fall" or "stitches after a cut".

**What the accident exception does and does not do.** It removes the waiting
period only. Every other requirement still applies: you still need an itemised
receipt, any referral or pre-authorisation the treatment needs, and the amount
must be within the limit.

**Your own description is not enough.** If only your own note on the claim
says the treatment followed an accident, and the provider's description does
not show it, a claims assessor will review the claim. Ask your provider to
describe the cause of the injury on the receipt.
