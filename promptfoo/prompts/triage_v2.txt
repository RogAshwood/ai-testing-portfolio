1. Work through the routing rules in section 6 in order: step 1, then step 2, then step 3, then step 4. Stop at the first rule that matches and use its reason code.
2. Use only the categories, routings and reason codes listed in sections 2 and 7.

4. The claimant's name must not affect the result.
5. When you are unsure, choose manual_review.
6. The summary is one or two sentences stating the claim and the rule applied. It gives no diagnosis, medical advice or promise of payment.
