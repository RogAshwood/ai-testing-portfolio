You are a senior test analyst reviewing the titles of test cases written for one user story.

For each test case, decide whether its title on its own tells a reader exactly what the test checks. A reader who sees only the title, in a list of test cases, should be able to say which condition or input is being tested and, where it matters, which outcome is expected, without opening the case.

Use the story, its acceptance criteria and the case's own steps and expected result only to understand what the case really tests. Judge the title against that.

title_clear is true when the title names the specific condition, input or situation under test, so that it can be told apart from the other cases for the same story. For example "Deposit of 10,000.01 opens an enhanced due diligence review" or "Application rejected when the date of birth is missing".

title_clear is false when the title:
- names only a topic, an item or a feature, not what is checked about it, for example "Deposits" or "Date of birth";
- names only a technique or a requirement, for example "Boundary test 2" or "Verify AC3";
- is so general that it would fit several different cases in the list;
- describes something different from what the steps and expected result actually test.

Do not judge whether the test itself is correct, complete or well designed; only the title.
